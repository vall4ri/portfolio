Swift

Web app

Capstone project UXR Pharma

1Problem statement

The Lean Daily Management (LDM) self-performance evaluation tool utilized by medical representatives and regional managers faces usability challenges, hindering its effectiveness and user adoption. Additionally, the cadences between users lack structure, occurring ad-hoc without clear objectives, thus impeding productive discussions and outcomes.

2Project aim

This project aims to enhance the user experience of the LDM tool and optimize cadences between users to improve overall performance management and outcomes.

Where

For the majority of this project, the main stakeholders and teammates for me were based in Russia. The requirements of design and strategy emerged from the Russian business sector in Dr. Reddy's. Holding over 50% market share in Emerging Markets, this was the largest base I had an opportunity to work with.

For who

The users are Medical Representatives (MRs)—they promote pharmaceutical products to healthcare professionals, ensuring compliance while driving product awareness, market access, and feedback.
They report to Regional Managers (RMs), who oversee brand growth, ensure sales efficiency, and aim to exceed performance targets for market expansion.

Research Methods

3

I used the Lean UX framework. This is a streamlined approach to UX research that focuses on quickly gathering insights and feedback to inform the design process. It emphasizes iterative testing, collaboration, and adapting designs based on user input, aiming to minimize waste and maximize efficiency in product development.

The essential part of conducting the user research was to hear from the users directly. Having the target user groups ready, framing the questions was segregated on the basis of ADKAR module— which divides the questions in Awareness, Desire, Knowledge. Ability and Reinforcement.

Data Synthesis

In the pursuit of refining processes and improving tool usability, there's an ongoing effort to integrate change management into cadences involving MRs and RMs. This is facilitated by the ADKAR framework, which simplifies the process and addresses resistance effectively. Based on the 1,500+ surveys gathered, this was the data:

20%

of the MRs have meetings set at no specific frequency.

55%

of the RMs have meetings on ad-hoc basis

20%

5Designing the app

Based on the researched and analysed data, the designs for Swift (previously LDM) were created. They would be executed into application in the following phases of the project.

Nudges introduced would help MRs know where they're lacking, and what to cater to first.

Now, the application would also provide a summary of data upfront; so the MRs do not need to view all KPIs.

Also, a KPI search option is now incorporated into the application.

6Business Impact

Swift, our redesigned performance evaluation tool, promises to transform the way our sales teams operate. With enhanced user experience and functionality, Swift streamlines performance evaluation for our 550 medical representatives and 200+ regional managers across Russia. Anticipating a 10% increase in sales productivity and at least a 15% growth in sales over the next year, Swift's intuitive design and enhanced features facilitate quicker decision-making and more targeted sales strategies. With the increased user adoption by 20%, Swift is not just a tool; it's a catalyst for success in the competitive world of pharmaceutical sales.
